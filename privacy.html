Dockit: The Doctor’s Kit
Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026 · Covers Dockit accounts, medicine-reminder sync and notifications

In short: Your health folders, notes, reports and trackers stay on your own phone. Our server holds only what is needed for your account, for password recovery, and for medicine reminders that you choose to share between a doctor and a patient. We don’t sell your data or show ads. Dockit is a notebook. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition.

What stays on your phone only

These are saved in the app’s own storage on your device and are not sent to our server:

  • Health folders, body-map marks, appointments, health numbers, vaccines and surgeries, visit notes.
  • Mood, meal, weight, sleep and habit entries.
  • Emergency card details, such as blood group, allergies and contacts.
  • Reports you add, as PDF or photo files.
  • In Professional mode, patient folders and notes that you enter.
  • Your folder password check and app-lock setting.

What our server stores

  • Account details: your email address, your chosen display name, whether you use Personal or Professional mode, and your sign-in credentials. Your password is handled by our sign-in service and stored only in scrambled (hashed) form. We cannot read it. Your email is used to sign you in and to recover your password.
  • Medicine reminders: when a doctor and a patient link their accounts, the medicine plan between them is stored on our server so both can see it and so reminders can be sent. This includes medicine names, doses and times as typed by the doctor or patient, and whether a dose was marked as taken.
  • Linking information: which doctor and patient accounts are linked, and invite links or QR codes used to link them. Either person can unlink at any time.
  • Notification token: a device code that lets us send a reminder to your phone.
  • Technical records: basic logs kept by our hosting provider, such as sign-in time, to keep the service running and secure.

Everything else stays private on your phone. It reaches another person only if you choose to send it, as described below.

Where it is stored, and who helps us run it

  • Server data is stored with [DATABASE PROVIDER] in the [SERVER REGION] region. They process it for us to run Dockit and do not use it for their own purposes.
  • To deliver reminders on Android, we use Google’s Firebase Cloud Messaging. It receives your notification token and the reminder text so it can reach your phone.
  • Data is encrypted while travelling between your phone and our server, and our provider encrypts stored data.
  • We don’t sell your data, and we don’t share it for advertising.

What leaves your phone only when you choose

  • Share with doctor, CSV export and backup files: when you tap these, Dockit creates a file or text and gives it to your phone’s share menu. You decide where it goes. After that, the app you send it to handles it under its own rules.
  • Phone calls: the Emergency button and helpline links open your phone’s dialler. Dockit does not make the call for you.

Notifications

Reminders arrive as phone notifications. You can turn them off at any time in your phone’s settings (Settings → Apps → Dockit → Notifications). Turning them off does not delete your account.

Ads, tracking and permissions

  • Dockit has no ads and no tracking or analytics tools.
  • The app loads the Nunito text font from Google Fonts. When it does, Google may receive your device’s IP address and basic request details, as with any web font. We plan to include the font inside the app so this request stops.
  • Dockit does not use your location, contacts, microphone or camera. You may pick a file or photo from your device to add as a report.

Professional mode

If you are a doctor or other professional, patient notes you type stay on your device only. Only medicine plans you choose to link with a patient’s account go to our server. You are responsible for having permission to keep patient information and for following the rules that apply to your work. Do not store identifiable patient information on a shared or unlocked phone.

Keeping your data safe

  • Folders open with your password and lock after 5 minutes. You can also turn on app lock.
  • Because most of your data lives only on your phone, uninstalling Dockit or clearing its data deletes it. Use Backup and restore to keep a copy. Anyone who has your unlocked phone and your password can see your data, so keep both safe.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account and reminder data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete it.

In the app: open Profile, scroll down and tap Delete my account and data, then confirm. This deletes your account and medicine reminders from our server, and your folders and saved report files from your phone. It cannot be undone.

Without opening the app: email [CONTACT EMAIL] from the address on your account and ask us to delete your account. We will delete your account and the server data linked to it. Data on your phone can be removed by uninstalling Dockit or clearing its storage (Settings → Apps → Dockit → Storage → Clear data).

You can also ask us to correct your account details, or to tell you what we hold about you, by writing to the same address.

Children

Dockit is not designed for children under 13. A parent or guardian should set up and supervise use by younger people.

Not medical advice

Dockit does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition and does not check medicine doses. It does not replace a doctor. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Changes

If we change how Dockit handles your data, we will update this policy and tell you in the app. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
