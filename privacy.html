Dockit: The Doctor’s Kit
Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026 · Covers Dockit online accounts, cloud sync and notifications

In short: With an online account, your Dockit data is saved on your phone and in your account on our server, so you can open it on any phone. It is protected in transit and in storage, but it is not end-to-end encrypted, which means the developer can technically access it. We don’t sell your data or show ads. You can delete everything at any time. Dockit is a notebook. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition.

Who we are

Dockit is made by Dockit (NEEV school start-up project).
Contact: dockitapp.in@gmail.com

What our server stores

When you use an online account, we store this for you:

  • Account details: your email address, your display name, whether you use Personal or Professional mode, and your sign-in credentials. Your password is handled by our sign-in service and stored only in scrambled (hashed) form. We cannot read it. Your email is used to sign you in and to recover your password.
  • Your Dockit data: your health folders, body-map marks, appointments, health numbers, vaccines and surgeries, visit notes, mood, meal, weight, sleep and habit entries, emergency card details (such as blood group, allergies and contacts), medicines and dose records, and your settings. In Professional mode this also includes the patient folders and notes you enter. This is saved as one record linked to your account.
  • Reviews: if you write a review, its stars, text, the name you choose to show and the date are stored on our server and are public: everyone using Dockit can read them. You can delete your review in the app at any time, and it is deleted if you delete your account.
  • Notification token: a device code that lets us send a reminder to your phone (when reminders are switched on).
  • Technical records: basic logs kept by our provider, such as sign-in time, to keep the service running and secure.

Report files (PDF or photos) that you add are, for now, kept on your phone only. If we start syncing them, we will update this policy first.

What is also kept on your phone

  • A copy of your data, so Dockit works offline. Changes sync when you are back online.
  • Your report files, your folder password check and your app-lock setting.
  • If you choose a local account instead of an online account, everything stays on your phone only and nothing is sent to our server.

How your data is protected, and who can see it

  • Your data is sent to our server over an encrypted connection, and our provider encrypts it while stored.
  • It is not end-to-end encrypted. That means the developer can technically access the database where it is stored.
  • We will not look at your health data except to keep Dockit working, to fix a problem you ask us to fix, or where the law requires it.
  • Each account can read only its own data. Other users cannot see it.
  • We don’t sell your data, and we don’t share it for advertising.

Where it is stored, and who helps us run it

  • Account and sync data is stored with Supabase in the Mumbai, India region. Supabase processes it for us to run Dockit and does not use it for its own purposes.
  • Emails such as password reset messages are sent through our sign-in service, or an email provider that works for us.
  • Reminder notifications (being added) are delivered with Google’s Firebase Cloud Messaging. It receives your notification token and the reminder text so it can reach your phone.
  • The Dockit app pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. When you open Dockit, GitHub may receive your device’s IP address and basic request details, as with any website.

Doctor and patient sharing

A doctor can link a patient’s folder to the patient’s own Dockit account, but only after the patient agrees by entering a one-time invite code. The code works once and expires after 2 days.

  • What we store for this: the link between the two accounts (including the doctor’s and patient’s display names), the medicine plans the doctor adds (medicine name, dose, times, dates and notes), and, for each dose, whether the patient marked it as taken.
  • What each person can see: the doctor and the patient can both see the medicine plans of an active link. Only the doctor can add or change them. The doctor can see which doses were taken or missed only while the patient keeps dose sharing switched on. The patient can switch it off at any time.
  • Ending a link: either person can end the link at any time. After that, the doctor can no longer see the patient’s plans or doses, and the plans are removed from the patient’s medicine list in the app.
  • Medicine plans and dose records are deleted from our server if you delete your account.

What leaves your phone only when you choose

  • Share with doctor, CSV export and backup files: when you tap these, Dockit creates a file or text and gives it to your phone’s share menu. You decide where it goes. After that, the app you send it to handles it under its own rules.
  • Phone calls: the Emergency button and helpline links open your phone’s dialler. Dockit does not make the call for you.

Notifications

Reminders arrive as phone notifications. You can turn them off at any time in your phone’s settings (Settings → Apps → Dockit → Notifications). Turning them off does not delete your account.

Ads, tracking and permissions

  • Dockit has no ads and no tracking or analytics tools.
  • The app loads the Nunito text font from Google Fonts. When it does, Google may receive your device’s IP address and basic request details, as with any web font. We plan to include the font inside the app so this request stops.
  • Dockit does not use your location, contacts, microphone or camera. You may pick a file or photo from your device to add as a report.

Professional mode

If you are a doctor or other professional, the patient folders and notes you enter are saved to your account on our server, like everything else. You are responsible for having permission to keep patient information and for following the rules that apply to your work. Dockit is a new pilot, so until we tell you otherwise, please use demo or dummy patient information only. Do not keep identifiable patient information on a shared or unlocked phone.

Keeping your data safe

  • Folders open with your password and lock after 5 minutes. You can also turn on app lock.
  • On a phone that is not yours, tick This is not my phone when you log in. Dockit then removes your data from that phone when you log out.
  • Anyone who has your unlocked phone and your password can see your data, so keep both safe.
  • With a local account, uninstalling Dockit or clearing its data deletes your data. Use Backup and restore to keep a copy.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account and data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete it.

In the app: open Profile, scroll down and tap Delete my account and data, then confirm. This deletes your account and all your data from our server, and your folders and saved report files from your phone. It cannot be undone. Copies in our provider’s backups, if any exist, can take a short time to disappear.

Without opening the app: email dockitapp.in@gmail.com from the address on your account and ask us to delete your account. We will delete your account and the server data linked to it. Data on your phone can be removed by uninstalling Dockit or clearing its storage (Settings → Apps → Dockit → Storage → Clear data).

You can also ask us to correct your account details, or to tell you what we hold about you, by writing to the same address.

Children

Dockit is not designed for children under 13. If you are under 18, please use Dockit only with the permission of a parent or guardian, who should help you set it up.

Not medical advice

Dockit does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any medical condition and does not check medicine doses. It does not replace a doctor. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Changes

If we change how Dockit handles your data, we will update this policy and tell you in the app. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
